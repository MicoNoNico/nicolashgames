# Version 0.6.0
June 9, 2025

## Features

**Game Rebrand!**
New title and logo.

**Unity Version Upgrade**
Unity version has been upgrade from 2022 to Unity 6. 
There are many improvements that come with the new version upgrade, but notably the game should now run a lot more efficiently on all CPU/GPUs.

**Emote Overhaul**
Reworked the emote system and added more emotes for various states of the customer.

**New Furniture: Play Tables**
A new furniture type has been added to the game: Play Tables. 
Tables are unlocked through the perk tree and can be used by the customers to play tabletop games at their own pace.
Once a customer is done playing, they will pay a fee based on the amount of time they played.
The same mechanics of the Play Tables now also apply to Arcade machines, albeit with a smaller play fee.

**Settings Overhaul**
The entire settings panel has been reworked. New tabs include:
- Game (Time format, language, etc.)
- Graphics (Resolution, screen mode, etc.)
- Audio (Volume, music, etc.)
- Controls (Controls rebinding.)

**Italian Localization Update**
Partially complete, around 60% of the game has been translated to Italian.


## Notable Bug Fixes & Improvements

- Fixed an exploit where the tutorial customer would pay any amount for their purchase.

- Fixed customers sometimes leaving without paying on day end.

- First iteration of review rebalance. Reviews should now be slightly more forgiving.

- You can now change an item's price directly from the inventory app in your phone.

- Nerfed marketing customer multiplier by about half. It was causing more customer spawns than intended.
