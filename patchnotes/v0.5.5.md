# Version 0.5.5.h2
May 23, 2025

## Notable Bug fixes & improvements

- Customers no longer get stuck if prices are too high. After a certain amount of attempts they will leave.

- Slightly increased dust pile and player colliders for better detection.

- Added new "Next Day" button instead of "Press N to proceed". This is to avoid input issues when typing.

- Fixed tabletop category not being shown in inventory manager app.

- Fixed tabletop and booster box count not being initialized properly when loading a save.

- Added category limits for shelves and tables. 
    *Now shelves only accept cards, booster boxes and video games, while tables accept tabletop and booster boxes.*

- Added "console" command to enable Unity's console logging.

- Slightly increased distance to npc entry/exit points.

# Version 0.5.5
May 17, 2025

## Features

**Tutorial Rework**
The tutorial has been remade to be more in depth.

**Perk Tree Restructure and Additions**
The perk tree has been restructured and moved to its own app in the phone.
Item perks have been streamlined into single categories and the bulk discount functionality perks moved to their own section.
This change drastically reduced the complexity of the perk tree.

New perks:
- Staff Cleaning. Restock and storage workers now use the broom to clean up dust piles if they are idle.
- Discounts. Bulk purchase discounts have been moved to their own perks.
- Bank Loans. Bank loans are now tied to perks. Unlock better loan options with better perks.
- Furniture. Furniture is now also tied to perks. Unlock better furniture with better perks.
- Tabletop. New sale item type: tabletop. See next section.

**Tabletop Sale Items**
A new item type is now available for unlock and sale in your store: tabletop games!
There are three tiers of tabletop games to unlock.

**New Furniture: Tables**
A new furniture type has been added to the game: tables. 
Tables are unlocked through the perk tree and can only be restocked with boxes.

**Store Robbers**
Your store may now be visited by unsavory individuals who will try to steal your items. 
Robbers that are running away can be punched to reclaim their stolen items!


## Notable Improvements

- The phone interface is now bigger.

- The experience bar is now back in the game.

- The perk app now shows a small notification number, indicating the amount of perk points left to spend.

- Added the option to toggle glass for walls adjacent to the street.

- Not purchased floor tiles now have a ceiling and furniture cannot be placed inside them anymore.

- Updated all interaction control popups to be more in depth.

- The warehouse rack UI now also displays icons for the box item type stored.

- Added 5 more music tracks to the game.


## Notable bug fixes

- Fixed items not being displayed in a proper grid during checkout.

- When an object snaps back to its original position, it will now also snap back to its original rotation.

- Reworked the music system to avoid fade and length syncing issues.
