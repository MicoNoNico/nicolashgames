# Version 0.6.3
July 4, 2025

## Features

**Store Rating Overhaul**
The store rating is now managed by an overall rating and three sub-ratings. The overall rating is the average of the three sub-ratings.

The sub-ratings each control a specific category:
- Variety. Based on the store's item variety.
- Efficiency. Based on the store's staff efficiency.
- Decoration. Based on the store's decoration/cleanliness.

In addition, you can now right-click on a customer to view what they are doing and their thoughts on the store.
All review conditions have been re-balanced, but may need further tuning.


**Walls and Floor Customization** 
You can now access new customization options from your store app in the phone.
The new customization options include:
- 10 wall colors.
- 3 floor textures.

*This feature is experimental and subject to change. More customization options will be added in future updates.*


**Italian Localization Update**
100% of the game has been translated to Italian.


## Notable Improvements

- Changed percentage buttons in price setting to 15% and 30%.
- Cashier workers now only leave when the queue is empty.
- Added an indicator to the cashier interface that shows the remaining queue.
- Added a notification counter to the build mode button that shows the amount of furniture in your inventory.
- Added a small animation to category buttons in the phone to better show the selected category.
- Collision system overhaul. Collisions are now a lot more accurate when placing or moving furniture.
- Added a small pop animation to furniture when placed.
- Customers will now not consider shelves in the warehouse area of the store.
- The amount of money a customer pays for play tables is now correctly deducted from their money to spend.


## Notable Bug Fixes

- Fixed the tutorial localization not updating at runtime.
- Fixed the furniture inventory not resizing to scroll horizontally when a lot of furniture is displayed.
- Fixed various text objects no resizing correctly.
- Fixed an issue that prevented rmb mouse input after going back to menu.
- Fixed a few issues that caused customers to get stuck in checkout queues.
- Fixed an issue that caused boxes to get stuck when the player and worker deposited at the same time.
- Fixed arcades not counting as decorations.
- Fixed some issues that prevented the decoration counter from updating correctly.
- Fixed ceilings not being properly activated/deactivated when loading a save file.
- Fixed trash piles not being deleted when switching to another save file.
