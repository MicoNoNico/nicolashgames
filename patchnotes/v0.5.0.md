# Version 0.5.0
April 9, 2025

## Features

**Perk Tree**
Players can now unlock passive perks to improve their store.
Perk bonuses have a wide range of effects, from increased item variety to staff efficiency and many more.
Perk points are acquired by leveling up the store.

**Losing Condition**
You can now lose!
Ending a day with a negative balance will immediately trigger a game over screen.
Getting a game over does not mean that the save file is deleted, you may still play the same day again to try and recover your balance.

**Bank Loans**
In a sticky situation? Get a loan from your trusty bank!
Added a bank section to the phone, from where you may choose a loan from three available options.
While repaying a loan, a specific amount will get deducted from your balance every day.
You may also choose to repay the loan in full at any point from the bank section of the phone.

**Utilities Expenses**
You will now need to pay a small amount of utilities expenses every day.
The expense increases as the store expands. 


## Notable bug fixes & improvements

- Furniture in the inventory that has not been placed is now saved correctly.

- Fixed some highlighting issues related to dust pile cleaning with the broom. 

- Furniture inventory UI now updates in real time if a new box is brought by a storage worker.

- Can now add to cart even while a delivery is in progress.

- Customers who can't find an available checkout will now show an angry emote.

- Adjusted leveling curve to support 20 levels instead of 10. 
(Levels are still infinite but by reaching level 20 the player will be able to unlock every perk currently in the game.)

- Many more small fixes and improvements!
