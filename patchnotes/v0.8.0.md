# v0.8.0

Turbishi

## Feature Additions

### Save Anywhere
- You can now save at any time from your phone, instead of only at the end of a day.
- Multiple save slots, managed from a new save panel on the phone. The game pauses while it is open.
- Saves now capture where you are standing and what you are carrying, and put you back exactly there on load.
- Mid-day saves now remember the whole shop floor: customers keep the items in their hands and their intent to check out, and workers keep their assigned role, their carried boxes and their cleaning duty.
- Each store saves separately, so running more than one shop no longer overwrites your progress.
- Autosave and the automatic end-of-day recap have been removed. The day's recap is now snapshotted at midnight and shown when you ask for it.

### Welcome to Meepleton — new tutorial
- A complete new opening mission that walks you through your first day.
- Tasks now use a live checklist, ticking off each condition as you complete it.
- Tutorial text types itself out, and completed tasks animate as they are checked off.
- Guided conditions cover moving and using the camera, buying stock, restocking shelves, furnishing the store and taking your first customer through checkout.
- The tutorial waits for its pointer target to actually exist before pointing at it, so it no longer highlights nothing.
- The skip-tutorial panel has been removed and tutorial startup for a new game is much cleaner.

### Licences and Branches
- New licence system with tiers, unlocked through XP progression.
- All licence categories are now in the game.
- New Branch panel for picking your specialisation, replacing the old perk node board.
- Licence categories, tiers and branch perks all have their own dedicated UI.

### Store Management PC
- The in-store PC now runs a full store management program.
- Customise your store's look from the PC: wall and floor materials are picked from browsable lists.
- New decor taskbar, showing the current time and your store's name live.
- Store lighting now runs on its own directional light, separate from the city.

### Traffic
- A full traffic system: cars drive the roads, obey intersections and stop at working traffic lights.
- Careful crossing the road, getting hit by a car now costs you energy.

### Maps
- New minimap with world icons and zoom controls, which follows you and toggles correctly as you enter and leave your store.
- New fullscreen city map with zooming and panning.

### Energy
- New energy bar. Energy drains as you move around and is carried through saves.

### Furniture store and shop NPCs
- New furniture store you can visit and buy from.
- Furniture you buy is delivered straight into your store's inventory, and inventory is now tracked per store.

### Deliveries
- New delivery scheduling system for ordering stock ahead of time.

### Running the shop
- You can now temporarily close your store, with a HUD showing your current open/closed state.
- New continuous day cycle, replacing the old stepped one.
- Walls fade out when they block your view, so you can always see your shop floor.
- New store PC furniture.
- New trash bin and broom holder models.
- New shelf models: Big, Medium, Small, and a double-sided simple shelf.
- New counter model.
- New store sign.

### Patch Notes
- In-game patch notes are now fetched from the web, so this list stays up to date without every new build.

### Console
- The Command Console now autocompletes commands with TAB.
- New `add_furniture` command.
- New `refresh_patchnotes` command.

## Improvements

- **Camera** — Reworked camera panning. Panning is now held on right mouse button instead of middle mouse. Build mode gets its own camera with boundaries based on your store's position.
- **Store entrances** — Walking in and out of your store is smoother, and the transition no longer depends on a single manager, so multiple stores work properly.
- **Shelves** — Big and Small shelves have new models and a rewritten script for finer control, with rebalanced item capacities. New medium shelf and a double-sided simple shelf. Shelf capacities were tuned again for the medium shelf.
- **Furniture placement** — Placement now respects each piece's true tile footprint, and furniture can no longer be placed over gaps in the floor.
- **Store layout** — Multiple passes on store size, layout, lighting and the counter model.
- **Starting out** — Your starter package now arrives in your inventory instead of being dropped into the world.
- **NPCs** — General optimisation pass on both customers and staff. Customers no longer waste time walking back to the entrance, and cleaning logic moved onto the shared worker base.
- **Vehicles** — Optimisation pass on car prefabs and traffic behaviour.
- **Performance** — Occlusion culling and LOD setup across every city building, so the new city runs well.
- **Audio** — The audio system now supports multiple freely-configurable sound channels.
- **Interface polish** — Reworked phone home screen and restock panel, plus several passes on licence, tutorial and general GUI layout.
- **Under the hood** — Upgraded to Unity 6.4, along with Localization, Timeline, Cinemachine and Recorder.

## World & Content

- **A whole new city.** The game moves to a completely rebuilt city built on a new art set, replacing the old scene.
- Multiple city blocks laid out and populated, including a park, with buildings grouped and set up for performance.
- New furniture store building and interior.
- New car models with brake lights and exhaust particles.
- New NPC portraits for the wholesaler, the furniture store owner and the HR representative.
- Traffic lights placed throughout the city.
- Asset and localisation naming pass across the project.
